# PY01 · Temperature converter

Create `main.py` with a `celsius_to_fahrenheit(celsius)` function. Convert Celsius to Fahrenheit and return the result. Keep input/output separate from the reusable conversion function.

Test 0 degrees, 100 degrees and -40 degrees. Decide how invalid input should be handled and explain that decision in your own `README.md` (at least 40 words). The dashboard checks valid Python, the named non-stub function, an arithmetic expression inside it, and your explanation. Those structural checks do not execute these tests.
